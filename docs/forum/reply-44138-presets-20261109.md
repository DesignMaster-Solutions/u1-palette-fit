Found them, thank you for the pointer. The colour selector of each filament on the official store prints the name and the hex together, as in your screenshot ("Pearl White (E2DEDB)"), so the preset is live now (commit 9317172, same URL).

**Snapmaker spools by name.** Under "Your spools" there is a new selector with the 120 colours of the ten lines the store lists: SnapSpeed PLA (30), Matte PLA (23), PETG HF (14), Silk PLA (12), PETG Translucent (9), TPU 95A HF (9), PLA-CF (7), PETG-CF (7), PLA Translucent (5) and PLA Glow (4). Pick one colour, or "All 23 Matte PLA colours" for a whole line, press Add, and the `name,#RRGGBB` lines are written for you. Nobody has to know a hex any more, which was your first point.

Three of the four model colours are Matte PLA values exactly, and the white that had no spool in reach on the four-spool shelf is now an exact match. With the SnapSpeed PLA line instead the same model gets Red 8.01, Black 2.08, Pearl White 7.15 and Yellow 2.31, so the plan also shows which line suits a model before buying anything.

**Source and limits.** The values were read on 9 November from https://us.snapmaker.com/products/snapspeed-pla-filament (that page lists every RFID line under the product) and are kept, with the date, in [`snapmaker_filaments.mjs`](https://github.com/DesignMaster-Solutions/u1-palette-fit/blob/main/snapmaker_filaments.mjs). Two new tests cover the table and the Santa case (`node --test` 17/17). These are the nominal values the store shows; a printed spool can differ from its hex. If a line or colour is missing, or a regional store shows a different value, tell me and I will correct the file.

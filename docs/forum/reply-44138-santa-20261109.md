Two things the real file confirmed. The object carries `extruder` 0 in `model_settings.config`, so the red body (60 cm²) prints in filament 1 without the file ever naming it: that is the bug you caught. And the white is the largest surface of the model, so for a shelf without white, "no spool in reach" is the honest answer rather than a forced red.

On grams versus cm²: your print profile on Snapmaker Space publishes the slicer's usage, 10.78 / 5.01 / 24.82 / 8.46 g. Same order as the surfaces, but the ratio runs from 0.16 to 0.29 g per cm² because walls and infill differ per colour. That is why the tool stays with surface and leaves grams to the slicer.
